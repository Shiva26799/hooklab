# Weekly Content Report: Dog Longevity

This report is based on an analysis of 26 reels (22 with usable data). Because this is a small sample size, these findings should be treated as **early signals** rather than absolute proof.

---

### 1. Make these 3 videos this week

**Video 1: The "How Old Was Your Dog" Recipe**
*   **The Hook:** "How old was your dog when you found out that three simple ingredients could help with their seasonal allergies?"
*   **The Script:** "I wish I knew this sooner. If you grate one whole apple, mix it with a spoonful of peanut butter and one egg, you get these. Just roll them into little balls and bake at 350 for 15 minutes. Not only do dogs love them, but they act like a natural antihistamine for those itchy paws. My dog thinks he’s just getting a treat, but I know it’s helping him feel better. Give it a try and let me know if your dog likes the taste."
*   **Format:** Voiceover over footage of you preparing the recipe and your dog eating it.
*   **The Data:** Based on Winner 1 (5,060 likes, 5,538 comments). This format drives massive engagement because it is helpful, easy to do, and features a "chef" dog.

**Video 2: The "Comeback" Story**
*   **The Hook:** "Six months ago, my dog could barely walk across the living room. Today, he’s a different dog."
*   **The Script:** "It’s hard watching them get older and lose their spark. We started focusing on gentle physical therapy and specific fitness movements—almost like dog pilates. It wasn't an overnight fix, but look at the difference in his posture and his confidence now. If you're struggling with a senior dog who's slowing down, don't lose hope. There are ways to help them get their mobility back. It just takes a little patience and the right routine."
*   **Format:** Voiceover over "Before" (struggling to walk) and "After" (moving well) footage.
*   **The Data:** Based on Winners 3 and 4 (combined 3,826 likes). Emotional "hope" stories perform exceptionally well with senior dog owners.

**Video 3: Breed-Specific Longevity Tips**
*   **The Hook:** "The [Breed Name] who live past 15 years old usually have one thing in common: they don't just eat kibble."
*   **The Script:** "If you have a [Breed], you know they can be prone to [specific issue, e.g., joint pain or skin issues]. To help them live longer, I add these three things to the bowl. First, blueberries for antioxidants. Second, sardines in water for those Omega-3s to help itchy skin. And third, a little plain pumpkin for digestion. These small additions can make a massive difference over a lifetime. What breed are you raising? Let me know in the comments."
*   **Format:** Fast-paced footage of adding the specific "superfoods" to a dog bowl.
*   **The Data:** Based on Winner 5 (1,356 likes, 84 comments). Specificity (mentioning a breed) makes the viewer feel the video was made just for them.

---

### 2. What is working
*   **Emotional Storytelling:** Content that shows a "transformation" or a "final wish" (Winner 2, 2,670 likes) resonates deeply. People want to feel hope for their aging pets.
*   **Actionable Recipes:** Simple, home-kitchen solutions for common problems like itchy skin or allergies are huge for engagement (Winner 1 had over 5,000 comments).
*   **Senior Care Focus:** 60% of the top-performing videos focused on senior dogs. This audience is highly motivated to find solutions.
*   **Voiceover on Footage:** This format made up 60% of the top group. It allows the dog to be the star while you provide the "expert" or "caring owner" perspective.
*   **Specific CTAs:** Asking a question like "How old was your dog when..." or "What breed do you have?" is driving the high comment counts.

---

### 3. What is not working
*   **Generic Hooks:** Vague titles like "Grey Muzzles, Golden Years" (Weak 1, 3 likes) fail to grab attention.
*   **The "Longevity Pill" Topic:** Videos focusing on "pills" or "news" about dog aging performed poorly (Weak 2 and 3, both 1 like). People seem skeptical of "magic bullets" and prefer natural, lifestyle-based tips.
*   **Text-Only/Carousel Styles:** These made up 40% of the bottom group. This audience wants to see real dogs in motion.
*   **No Call to Action:** 100% of the bottom-performing reels had no call to action. If you don't ask people to share or comment, they won't.

---

### 4. Two more video ideas
*   **The "Nightly Routine" for Longevity:** Show the 3 things you do every night to help your dog live longer (e.g., teeth brushing, joint supplement, 5 minutes of massage).
*   **"Stop Doing This":** A video about common habits that might be shortening a dog's life (e.g., overfeeding treats, skipping dental care) to spark "curiosity" and "shock."

---

### 5. Health claims to check before posting
The following claims appeared in the top-performing data. To build trust and avoid being flagged, you should cite a veterinary study or a specific vet's advice when mentioning:
*   Apples/Peanut Butter/Eggs acting as a "natural antihistamine."
*   Blueberries specifically "helping dogs live longer."
*   Sardines "calming itchy skin."
*   Fitness training/Pilates "curing" mobility issues.

---

### 6. Honest limits
This report is based on only **22 usable reels**. This is a very small sample size. While the preference for "senior care" and "storytelling" is strong, we need more data to see if "diet" tips consistently outperform "exercise" tips. These are early trends, not set-in-stone rules.

---

### 7. What could work next
*   **Test "Nostalgia":** Only 20% of top reels used nostalgia. Try using a trending "older" song with a montage of a dog growing up to see if it triggers more shares.
*   **The "Vet Voice":** While "Owner" perspective is winning now, testing a "What I wish my vet told me" angle could bridge the gap between being a peer and being an authority.
*   **Ultra-Short Tips:** 40% of winners were under 15 seconds. Try a "Rapid Fire" 10-second tip video (e.g., "One food to never give your senior dog").
