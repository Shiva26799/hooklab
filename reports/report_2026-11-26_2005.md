# Weekly Content Report: Dog Longevity

This report is based on an analysis of 26 reels (22 with usable data). Because the sample size is small, these findings should be viewed as **early signals** rather than absolute proof.

---

### 1. Make these 3 videos this week

#### **Video 1: The "Kitchen Remedy" Recipe**
*   **Hook (0–2s)**: (On-screen text: "Stop the paw licking 🍎") "How old was your dog when you found out that these three kitchen staples work like a natural antihistamine?"
*   **The Script**: "If your dog is constantly scratching or licking their paws, try this. Take one whole apple—grated—and mix it with a big spoonful of peanut butter and one egg. Roll them into little bite-sized balls and bake them at 350 for about 15 minutes. You’ve just made a treat that helps calm itchy skin and tastes like a cheat meal. My dog obsessed with these, and it’s way better than buying processed chews. Save this for the next time the allergies kick in."
*   **Video Direction**: POV shot of you grating the apple and mixing the ingredients in a bowl. Cut to a close-up of the dog waiting patiently (sitting or drooling). Show the finished treats coming out of the oven. End with the dog taking a gentle bite.
*   **Audio Direction**: Warm, helpful voiceover. Use ASMR sounds: the crunch of the apple, the clink of the spoon against the bowl, and the sound of the oven timer. Background music: Upbeat, acoustic folk.
*   **The Data**: Based on **Winner 1** (5,060 likes, 5,538 comments). This format had the highest engagement score (10,598) because it solved a specific problem with a simple DIY.

#### **Video 2: The "Age is Just a Number" Story**
*   **Hook (0–2s)**: (On-screen text: "He’s 15 and still going 🐾") "They told me he’d be slowing down by now, but six months ago we started one simple habit."
*   **The Script**: "This is [Dog's Name]. He’s 15 years old. Last year, he could barely make it around the block without stopping. It’s hard watching your best friend get old, but we didn't give up. We started focusing on low-impact movement and specific senior stretches every morning. Look at him now. He’s not a puppy, but he’s comfortable, he’s happy, and he’s still here. If you’re worried about your senior dog, don’t lose hope. There is so much we can do to help them feel better step by step."
*   **Video Direction**: A montage of "slow" moments—the dog sleeping, then waking up. Transition to "active" moments—the dog walking on grass, doing a gentle stretch, or wagging their tail. Use soft, natural lighting.
*   **Audio Direction**: Emotional, sincere voiceover. Background music: Emotional piano or a slow, nostalgic acoustic track.
*   **The Data**: Based on **Winner 2** (2,670 likes) and **Winner 3** (2,148 likes). Story-based hooks made up 60% of the top-performing group.

#### **Video 3: The "Breed Secret" (Specific Superfoods)**
*   **Hook (0–2s)**: (On-screen text: "How [Breed] live to 16+ 🫐") "The [Breed Name] who live past 15 don't just eat plain kibble. They usually have these three things in their bowl."
*   **The Script**: "If you want your [Breed] to stay healthy into their late teens, you have to look at their bowl. First: Blueberries. They are tiny brain boosters. Second: Sardines in water. Great for heart health and a shiny coat. Third: Plain pumpkin. It’s a lifesaver for their digestion. You don't need a total diet overhaul—just start adding these 'toppers' to their regular meals. Which one does your dog love most? Let me know in the comments."
*   **Video Direction**: Start with a shot of the specific breed looking at the camera. Then, show a "bowl prep" where you drop blueberries, a sardine, and a dollop of pumpkin onto dry kibble. Use on-screen text labels for each food item.
*   **Audio Direction**: Direct and informative tone. Sound of a can opening (sardines) and the "plop" of the pumpkin. Background music: Lo-fi beat.
*   **The Data**: Based on **Winner 5** (1,356 likes, 84 comments). Breed-specific advice and "shock" hooks (e.g., "don't just eat kibble") drive high curiosity.

---

### 2. What is working
*   **Storytelling Hooks**: 60% of the top-performing reels used a story (like Winner 2 and 3). People connect with the journey of a specific dog more than generic advice.
*   **Senior Care & Diet**: These two topics dominated the top group (60% senior care, 40% diet). Owners are highly motivated to help their aging pets.
*   **Problem/Solution Recipes**: Winner 1 (5,060 likes) shows that providing a "recipe" for a common problem (allergies) is a massive engagement driver.
*   **Specific Breed Targeting**: Mentioning a specific breed (like Chihuahuas in Winner 5) makes the content feel personalized and shareable.

### 3. What is not working
*   **The "Longevity Pill" Topic**: This made up 60% of the bottom group. It may feel too "salesy" or experimental for an audience looking for trust.
*   **No Call to Action (CTA)**: 100% of the bottom-performing reels had no CTA. If you don't tell people to comment or follow, they won't.
*   **Generic News/Updates**: "Happy Sunday" or "News" style hooks (Weak 3) failed to get any engagement (1 like, 0 comments).
*   **Text-Only/Carousels**: These formats were overrepresented in the bottom group (40%). People want to see the dogs and hear the owner's voice.

### 5. Health claims to check before posting
The following claims appeared in the top-performing data. To build trust, you should cite a veterinary study or a specific vet’s advice in your caption:
*   *Apple/PB/Egg treats work as a natural antihistamine.*
*   *Sardines in water calm itchy skin.*
*   *Blueberries help dogs live longer/boost brain health.*
*   *Specific "Pilates" or fitness moves can restore mobility in paralyzed or stiff dogs.*

---

### 6. Honest limits
*   **Sample Size**: We only looked at 22 usable reels. This is a small snapshot of the "dog longevity" niche.
*   **Algorithm Variance**: Some reels may have performed well because they were pushed to a specific "dog lover" interest group, not necessarily because the hook was perfect.
*   **Missing Data**: We don't have "Watch Time" data, which is the biggest factor in growth. We are guessing success based on Likes and Comments.

---

### 7. What could work next
*   **Testing "Nostalgia"**: One top reel used nostalgia. Try a "Then vs. Now" video of a senior dog to see if the emotional hook drives more shares.
*   **The "Vet Reacts" Style**: Since "Trust" is the goal, try a split-screen video where you show a healthy habit and explain *why* it works scientifically.
*   **Longer Educational Content**: 20% of top reels were over 60 seconds. Try one "Deep Dive" on a single ingredient (like Turmeric or Fish Oil) to see if your audience wants more detail.
