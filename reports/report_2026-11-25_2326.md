## 1. Make These 3 Videos This Week

### Video 1: Simple 3-Ingredient Allergy & Joint Treat
* **Hook Line (0–2 seconds):** "How old was your dog when you found out you can make an allergy treat at home with 3 simple ingredients?"
* **Exact Format:** Voiceover over close-up footage of making a simple treat and feeding it to a happy dog.
* **Complete Script (30–40 seconds):**
  > "How old was your dog when you found out you can make an allergy treat at home with 3 simple ingredients?  
  > All you need is one grated apple, a spoonful of dog-safe peanut butter, and one egg. Roll them into small balls and bake at 350 for 15 minutes.  
  > Apples give your dog antioxidants, while the healthy fats support their coat. It is a quick snack your dog will love, and it costs almost nothing.  
  > Have you ever tried baking for your dog? Tell me in the comments!"
* **Data Proving Why It Works:** Recipe and diet content drives huge engagement. Winner 1 used a similar recipe hook and pulled **5,060 likes and 5,538 comments**. Winner 5 also focused on simple food additions and got **1,356 likes and 84 comments**.

## 2. What Is Working

* **Personal Stories Drive Emotion and Shares:** Story hooks represent **60% of top-performing videos**. Videos sharing senior dog journeys, like Winner 2 (**2,670 likes, 92 comments**) and Winner 3 (**2,148 likes, 34 comments**), deeply resonate with dog owners.
* **Practical Diet & DIY Tips Trigger Huge Comment Numbers:** Simple recipes and food advice drive high interaction. Winner 1 generated **5,060 likes and 5,538 comments**, while Winner 5 brought in **1,356 likes and 84 comments**. Owners love easy, actionable meal upgrades.
* **Focusing on Practical Senior Care Over Speculative Topics:** Top reels focused heavily on senior care (**60%**) and diet (**40%**). Real-world tips on mobility and nutrition consistently outshine abstract science.
* **Asking Clear Questions in the Video:** **40% of top videos** included an explicit call for comments, driving strong viewer conversation (e.g., Winner 1’s 5,500+ comments).

---

## 3. What Is Not Working

* **Abstract "Longevity Pill" Content Flops:** Speculative news about longevity drugs accounted for **60% of bottom-performing reels**. Weak 2 ("Can we give our dogs more healthy years?") only received **1 like and 0 comments**. Viewers want practical care today, not abstract promises.
* **Vague Intros and General Greetings:** Opening a video with generic statements fails to hold attention. Weak 3 ("Happy Sunday, November 15th...") got **1 like and 0 comments**, and Weak 1 ("Grey Muzzles, Golden Years") got **3 likes and 0 comments**.
* **Videos Over 60 Seconds Without Strong Hooks:** Long videos made up **40% of the lowest group**. Without a strong hook in the first 2 seconds, long videos drop off quickly.
* **Skipping the Call to Action:** **100% of bottom reels** had no call to action, missing out on easy comment and share signals.

## 5. Health Claims to Check Before Posting

To build trust as an authoritative channel, avoid making absolute medical promises. Vet all health claims carefully.

* **Claims Seen in the Data:**
  * Treat recipes acting as a "natural antihistamine" or stopping scratching.
  * Blueberries or specific foods directly "helping dogs live longer."
  * Specific physical exercises or foods "curing" mobility issues.
  * Sardines "calming itchy skin."

* **Action Required:**
  * Frame foods as **supportive additions** rather than medical cures (e.g., "supports skin health" instead of "stops itching instantly").
  * Cite veterinary sources or consult a vet before stating that a ingredient acts like medication (such as an antihistamine).

## 6. Honest Limits of This Data

* **Small Sample Size:** This report is based on **26 analyzed reels (22 with complete data)**. Because the sample size is small, these insights should be viewed as **early signals**, not final proof.
* **Unmeasured Factors:** The dataset does not account for posting times, account follower counts, background music trends, or algorithmic distribution changes. Continue testing to confirm what works best for your specific audience.
