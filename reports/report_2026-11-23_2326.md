# Weekly Content Report: Dog Longevity

## 1. What is working
* **Showing professional authority:** The reel featuring a vet in a clinic setting hit a **110.000 engagement rate**, while the text-only video reached only **28.000**.
* **Specific, list-based hooks:** Framing the video as a clear checklist ("10 wellness tips that could add years to your dog’s life") brought in far higher engagement than a plain topic title.
* **Save CTAs over link CTAs:** Asking viewers to "save this list" performed much better than asking viewers to click a link in an under-15-second video.
* **Short, hopeful messaging:** Both reels were under 15 seconds and focused on hope, which keeps the tone encouraging for dog owners looking to improve their dog's life.

---

## 2. What is not working
* **Text-only screen overlays without authority:** Stripping out professional presence led to the lowest engagement in the test (28.000 engagement rate).
* **Vague hooks:** Simple titles like "Guide to Senior Dog Care" failed to give viewers a compelling reason to stop scrolling or save the video.
* **Asking for link clicks on ultra-short content:** Using a link CTA on a fast, under-15-second video yielded lower overall engagement compared to direct save prompts.

## 3. Five video ideas

### Idea 1: The Simple Weight Check That Adds Years
* **Title:** The Rib Test for Dog Longevity
* **Hook Line (0–2s):** "Here is a 5-second test to see if your dog is at a healthy weight."
* **Script (30–40 seconds):**
  > "Run your hands gently over your dog’s ribcage. You should easily feel their ribs under a thin layer of fur, just like feeling the back of your hand. If you have to press hard to feel them, your dog might be carrying extra weight. Keeping your dog lean can add up to two extra years to their life. Save this video so you can check your dog tonight."
* **Best Format:** Voiceover over video of a vet or owner demonstrating the rib check on a dog in a clinic or clear indoor setting. End with text on screen asking to save.

### Idea 2: The Daily Dental Trick
* **Title:** Why Toothbrushing Protects Your Dog’s Heart
* **Hook Line (0–2s):** "If your dog’s breath smells terrible, don't just ignore it."
* **Script (30–40 seconds):**
  > "Bad breath is often the first sign of gum disease. When bacteria build up along the gum line, they can enter the bloodstream and put extra strain on your dog's major organs over time. Brushing their teeth even three times a week makes a huge difference. Save this reminder to start a routine tonight."
* **Best Format:** Voiceover over footage of a dog getting its teeth brushed, shot in a bright visual style.

### Idea 3: The Gut Health Swap
* **Title:** Why You Shouldn't Feed the Exact Same Food Every Day
* **Hook Line (0–2s):** "Should your dog eat the exact same food every single day?"
* **Script (30–40 seconds):**
  > "A lot of owners stick to one kibble forever, but rotating safe toppers or protein sources can actually support a healthy gut. A diverse gut microbiome helps your dog absorb nutrients better and stay strong as they age. Try adding a spoon of plain pumpkin or goat milk this week. Save this tip for your next grocery run."
* **Best Format:** Voiceover showing healthy, simple food toppers being added to a dog bowl.

### Idea 4: 3 Early Signs of Aging to Catch Early
* **Title:** Catch Senior Dog Signs Early
* **Hook Line (0–2s):** "Three quiet signs your dog is starting to slow down."
* **Script (30–40 seconds):**
  > "First, hesitate before jumping into the car. Second, sleeping slightly longer after normal walks. Third, keeping their head lower when walking. Catching these early lets you adjust their joint support and exercise before pain starts. Save this checklist to track your dog's habits this month."
* **Best Format:** Voiceover over soft, real footage of older dogs walking and resting calmly.

### Idea 5: The Joint Check After Every Walk
* **Title:** How to Protect Your Dog’s Joints
* **Hook Line (0–2s):** "Do this quick check right after your dog’s afternoon walk."
* **Script (30–40 seconds):**
  > "Watch your dog closely for the first ten minutes after they sit down from a walk. Do they struggle to stand back up, or lick at their front paws? Stiffness right after rest is a key sign of joint soreness. Catching joint stress early helps you protect their mobility for life. Save this tip to check them today."
* **Best Format:** Voiceover over footage of a dog settling onto a dog bed after a walk.

## 4. Health claims to check before posting

Before publishing videos on these topics, verify the exact sources for these claims:

1. **"Maintaining a healthy weight can add 2+ years to a dog's life"**
   * *What needs a source:* You need to cite veterinary research (such as long-term canine lifespan and body condition studies) to back up the exact "2+ years" statistic.
2. **"Brushing teeth prevents gum disease which is linked to major health issues"**
   * *What needs a source:* You need a clear veterinary medical reference showing the link between canine periodontal disease and systemic organ stress (such as heart or kidney issues).
3. **"Diet rotation supports gut microbiome diversity"**
   * *What needs a source:* You need veterinary nutrition backing demonstrating that safe food rotation or toppers measurably improve gut flora diversity in dogs.

## 5. Honest limits

* **Very small sample size:** This report is based on analyzing only **2 reels total** (1 top performer, 1 bottom performer). 
* **Early signals, not proof:** Because the dataset is so small, these conclusions are early signals to test, not definitive proof of what works long-term.
* **Missing view metrics:** View counts were listed at 0 in the dataset, so performance relies strictly on relative engagement rate differences (110.000 vs 28.000).
* **What we cannot prove yet:** We cannot prove how these formats affect overall viewer retention, long-term subscriber conversion, or total channel reach until more videos are posted and tracked.
